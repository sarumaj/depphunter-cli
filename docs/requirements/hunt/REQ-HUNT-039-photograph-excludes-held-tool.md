## Statement

A photograph **shall not** contain the camera, the hands or anything else the
walker holds.

## Rationale

The held tool is drawn in a pass of its own, so leaving it out is a matter of
not making that pass; a camera cannot be in its own picture.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A photograph shows nothing of the camera or the hand.
