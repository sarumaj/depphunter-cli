## Statement

A bug beyond the tracker's range **shall** be drawn as an arrow on the tracker's
rim, pointing in the bug's direction.

## Rationale

The point of the tracker is to say which way to walk.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The tracker points at a bug on the far side of the map.
