## Statement

A caught bug **shall** leave the map by an animation specific to the tool that
caught it: bubbled, it rises sealed in soap until both vanish; reeled, it comes
down the line to the walker; netted, it is scooped by the net; pinned, it is
driven flat onto its surface; foamed, it sags and is buried; photographed, it
blinks out.

## Rationale

The tools are the whole character of walk mode; a catch that simply blinks out
says nothing about what took it.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Each tool takes a bug off the map in its own way.
2. A bubbled bug rises, a pinned or foamed one sinks and a reeled one is drawn
   in.
3. Every take finishes and leaves nothing behind.
