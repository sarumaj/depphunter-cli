## Statement

In the galaxy style, the system **shall** place crystals, glowing rubble and
beacons where the city has trees, bushes and lamps, and **shall** draw nebulae
and a star field instead of the sea and the sky.

## Rationale

The environment belongs to the style.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A galaxy map shows beacons on its terrace edges and stars in its water; walk
   mode shows a star field and nebulae instead of a sky.
