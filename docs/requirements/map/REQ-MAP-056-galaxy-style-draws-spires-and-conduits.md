## Statement

In the galaxy style, the system **shall** draw buildings as faceted crystal
spires, dark at the foot and lit towards the tip, with strata of light across
them and windows that read as stars, and **shall** draw a glowing conduit down
every gap between them.

## Rationale

The galaxy dresses the same geometry as light in the dark.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A galaxy map shows lit strata on its spires and glowing lines down its
   streets.
