## Statement

The view **shall** rise and fall with the heave of a jet holding the walker up
and with the swell under a pair of floats, and **shall not** bob with footsteps.
This motion **shall** be added to the eye height only, not to reach, aim or
where a shot leaves from.

## Rationale

A bob on every footfall is what makes people put a first-person view down;
aiming and reach must not wander with the view.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The view breathes under a jet and settles on solid ground.
2. Walking on the street causes no vertical view bob.
