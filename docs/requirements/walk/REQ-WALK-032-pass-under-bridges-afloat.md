## Statement

A bridge deck more than a step above the walker's feet **shall not** be treated
as ground under the walker, so a walker on the water passes beneath it.

## Rationale

A deck more than a step above the body is a deck the body is under; lifting the
walker onto every deck they passed beneath was wrong.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Swimming the water in the swim ring passes under a bridge instead of over it.
