## Statement

A walker **shall** be able to step down from a shore into the bay like off a
curb (a drop of at most 0.6 units), but **shall not** walk off a bridge deck or
off anything else standing well above the surface into the water; from there the
bay **shall** have to be jumped into.

## Rationale

Walking off an edge into a drop is not a thing anybody means to do, and a
railing is there to be gone over rather than through.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A walker without skimmers can walk off a shore into the bay and drowns unless
   they reach a shore again.
2. A bridge deck cannot be walked off into the water; a jump off it lands in the
   water.
