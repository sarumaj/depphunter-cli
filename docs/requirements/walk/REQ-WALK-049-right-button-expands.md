## Statement

In walk mode the right mouse button and `E` **shall** expand or collapse the
building the reticle is on.

## Rationale

The original walk mode could change the map's depth from the street.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Superseded; no longer verified.

## Notes

Superseded: collapsing folded buildings into a district block, which
looked like buildings vanishing, and rebuilt the city around the walker. The
right button now scopes and expanding is not offered in walk mode.
