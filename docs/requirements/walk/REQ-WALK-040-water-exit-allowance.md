## Statement

A walker on the ground at water level, swimming or in the water, **shall** be
able to climb a ledge 0.25 units higher than the normal step height, enough to
get up onto a shore standing 0.45 units above the surface.

## Rationale

The shore stands further above the surface than a step; without an allowance
walking out over the bay was one way.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A walker swimming in the ring can step ashore.
2. A walker in the water without the ring can step ashore before drowning.

## Notes

The allowance belongs to the water, not to the ring, so wearing it in a
street does not climb higher walls.
