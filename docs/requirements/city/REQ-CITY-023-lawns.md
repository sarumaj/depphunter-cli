## Statement

Lawns **shall** be one green with gentle large-scale variation, without dark
blotches, and park lawns **shall** carry mowing stripes.

## Rationale

The earlier dark blotches read as holes.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Shores show an even green; parks show mowing stripes.
