## Statement

The top 0.35 units of a ramp **shall** be level and closed by a barrier; from
the landing a driveway **shall** cross the terrace's sidewalk into its ring
road, and at the ramp's foot an apron **shall** replace the street's curb, so
both ends join the carriageways.

## Rationale

Both ends of a ramp must meet a road rather than a curb.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Driving up a ramp reaches the ring road on top without crossing a curb.
