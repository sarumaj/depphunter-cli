## Statement

On a ramp the walker's height **shall** follow the ramp surface, rising linearly
along the sloped part and level on its landing.

## Rationale

A ramp that the walker does not climb is scenery.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Walking up a ramp raises the view smoothly to the terrace's level.
