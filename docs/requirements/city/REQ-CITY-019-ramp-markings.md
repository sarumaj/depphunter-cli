## Statement

A ramp's roadway **shall** carry edge lines and chevrons pointing uphill, and
its outer side **shall** have a wall with a parapet.

## Rationale

The markings say which way is up and that the ramp is a road.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A ramp shows edge lines, uphill chevrons and a parapet.
