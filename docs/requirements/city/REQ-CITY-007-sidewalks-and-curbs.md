## Statement

Every obstacle (child footprint and terrace edge) **shall** be bordered by a
sidewalk with a curb.

## Rationale

Sidewalks separate buildings from the carriageway and make streets read as
streets.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Every building on a terrace has a sidewalk with a curb along it.
