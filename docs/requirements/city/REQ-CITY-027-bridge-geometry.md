## Statement

A bridge **shall** be an arched deck 0.8 units wide, rising 0.25 units at its
middle and meeting both shores at their level, with railings, piers every 1.8
units down to the water and a marked carriageway.

## Rationale

A recognizable bridge that meets the shores at their level can be walked on and
off without a step.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The deck's middle stands above the shores and its ends meet them.
2. A bridge shows railings, piers and a centre line.
