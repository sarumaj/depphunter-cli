## Statement

A square of park lawn between its gravel paths (REQ-CITY-010) that is lawn all
over - clear of the block's edge and of everything standing on the block by the
street's width - **shall**, about one time in six, hold an amenity of the map's
style, chosen and turned by its position so a map dresses the same every time:

- in a city, a five-a-side pitch with its goals, a basketball court with its
  hoops, or a playground with swings, a slide and a seesaw in a sandpit;
- on a circuit board, a pin header, a heat sink on its chip, or a coin cell in
  its holder beside a crystal;
- in the galaxy, a landing pad with lamps at its corners, a dish listening to
  the sky, or a ring of standing stones round a crystal; the lamps, the dish's
  feed and the crystal **shall** glow.

Nothing **shall** be planted on an amenity, and its posts - goalposts, a hoop's
pole, a swing's legs, a stone - **shall** stand in the walker's way.

## Rationale

A park was lawn, trees and bushes, the same in every block; a city's green
spaces are where it plays, and a board and a galaxy have their own things
standing in their open ground.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A large park in each style shows all three of its amenities, and still
   mostly lawn.
2. No amenity stands on a street, and no tree or bush stands on an amenity.
3. The walker cannot walk through a goalpost, a hoop's pole or a standing
   stone.
