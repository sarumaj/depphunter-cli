## Statement

On a bridge the walker's height **shall** follow the arch, and the deck
**shall** carry the walker only while the whole body (radius 0.12) is within the
deck. Its railings **shall** keep a walker on the deck whose feet are below their
top (0.14 above the deck) from leaving it over the side, on the ground or in the
air; only a jump that clears them **shall** go over. The ends, on the shores,
stay open.

## Rationale

Asking at the body's corners let a walker hang over the railing. The railings
held only a walker on the ground: a hop, or a stride down the arch that left the
deck for a frame - as walking downhill does - let the next step go through them
into the water.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Walking across a bridge rises and falls with the arch.
2. A walker cannot be walked out through the railing, down the arch or with a
   hop that stays below its top.
3. A jump that clears the railing goes over it.
