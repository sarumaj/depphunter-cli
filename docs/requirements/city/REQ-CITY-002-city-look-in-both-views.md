## Statement

The isometric map **shall** draw the same facades, roofs, streets, lawns, trees,
bushes, lamps, ramps and bridges as walk mode; walk mode **shall** add a sky
dome, the planet's water surface and the planet's curve.

## Rationale

One look in both views keeps the map and the street recognizably the same place.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The isometric map shows streets, trees and lamps.
2. Walk mode shows a sky and water around the map.
