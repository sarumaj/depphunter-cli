## Statement

Lamps **shall** stand on the sidewalk along every terrace edge, 1.6 units apart,
except where a ramp's driveway crosses the sidewalk.

## Rationale

Lamps mark the ring roads and light the city at night; one standing in a
driveway would block it.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Lamps line every terrace edge.
2. No lamp stands in a ramp's driveway.
