## Statement

A city's park **shall** hold playgrounds of four kinds, about as often
together as a pitch:

- swings, a slide and a seesaw in a sandpit;
- a roundabout, two spring riders and a climbing dome;
- a tower slide under a roof, a nest swing and a seesaw;
- a pipe wound once round its axis from a tower down to the ground, and two
  bobby cars.

Everything there is to play in a city - every ride, every court and its ball -
**shall** have its counterpart in each other style, built of that style's own
parts and played the same way, so that no style has a game another lacks.

A circuit board **shall** build them of its own parts - swings of wire from a
bus bar on standoffs with chips for seats, a slide of ribbon cable, a seesaw
that is a rocker switch, a roundabout that is an electrolytic capacitor (its
can the deck, its sleeve striped, its top scored with a vent), riders that are
transistors (horses of their black bodies with a lead bent up for a neck and
handlebar, one back for a tail, and the middle one anchored in a solder pad,
rocking on it), a pipe of heat-shrink sleeving banded in copper and cars in
solder mask - and its pitch, court and volleyball court of a copper pour and
solder mask, with goals of jumper wire, hoops that are test-point loops and a
net of ribbon cable. The galaxy **shall** build them of dark alloy and light:
pods for seats, arches for goals, a ring of light for a rim, a curtain of light
for a net and a pipe ringed with light, the lights glowing, and its bobby cars
finned.

Whatever on one moves when it is played - a swing's seat, a roundabout's deck,
a seesaw's plank, a rider on its spring, a car - **shall** be drawn apart from what
stands still, so that the one being played can move while the rest stand as
they are.

## Rationale

One playground repeated across a map read as a pattern. The other styles had
only things to stand round, so what could be played in a city could not be
played anywhere else - and once they had playgrounds, each style had some the
others did not, so which games there were depended on a setting chosen for its
looks.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Each style has the same four playgrounds and the same three courts, each
   played the same way and built of the style's own parts: a bobby car looks
   different in each.
2. Every swing, roundabout, seesaw and rider is drawn in its place at rest,
   and posing one moves that one only.
