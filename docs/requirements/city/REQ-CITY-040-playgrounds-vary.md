## Statement

A city's park **shall** hold playgrounds of three kinds, about as often
together as a pitch:

- swings, a slide and a seesaw in a sandpit;
- a roundabout, two spring riders and a climbing dome;
- a tower slide under a roof, a nest swing and a seesaw.

A circuit board **shall** have the first two built of its own parts - swings of
wire from a bus bar on standoffs with chips for seats, a slide of ribbon cable,
a seesaw that is a rocker switch, a roundabout that is an electrolytic
capacitor (its can the deck, its sleeve striped, its top scored with a vent),
riders that are transistors (horses of their black bodies with a lead bent up
for a neck and handlebar, one back for a tail, and the middle one anchored in a
solder pad, rocking on it) - and its pitch, court and volleyball court of a
copper pour and solder mask, with goals of jumper wire, hoops that are
test-point loops and a net of ribbon cable. The galaxy **shall** have the same
built of dark alloy and light: pods for seats, arches for goals, a ring of
light for a rim and a curtain of light for a net, the lights glowing. And a
playground of its own: a pipe ringed with light, wound once round its axis from
a tower down to the ground, and two bobby cars.

Whatever on one moves when it is played - a swing's seat, a roundabout's deck,
a seesaw's plank, a rider on its spring, a car - **shall** be drawn apart from what
stands still, so that the one being played can move while the rest stand as
they are.

## Rationale

One playground repeated across a map read as a pattern. The other styles had
only things to stand round, so what could be played in a city could not be
played anywhere else.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A large city park shows all three kinds of playground and the volleyball
   court; a board and a galaxy each show their pitch, court, volleyball court
   and two playgrounds, and the galaxy its pipe slide and bobby cars.
2. Every swing, roundabout, seesaw and rider is drawn in its place at rest,
   and posing one moves that one only.
