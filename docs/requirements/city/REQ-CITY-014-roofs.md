## Statement

Flat roofs **shall** be gravel with a parapet and carry either a plant room with
air-conditioning units or, on roofs at least 0.6 units across, rows of solar
panels.

## Rationale

Roofs are most of what the isometric view shows of a building.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Roofs show both plant rooms and solar panels across a large map.
