## Statement

Flat roofs **shall** be gravel inside a parapet that shades them, and carry
what the building's type (REQ-CITY-031) does: a sawtooth of north lights on a
warehouse; a plant penthouse and a window-cleaning track on an office, and a
helipad on one at least 7 units high; stepped rings and a spire base on an
art-deco tower; tar, chimneys and a timber water tank on brick; beds of sedum on
some residential roofs; and otherwise a plant room with air-conditioning units
or, on roofs at least 0.6 units across, rows of solar panels.

## Rationale

Roofs are most of what the isometric view shows of a building.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Roofs show plant rooms, solar panels, sawtooth roofs, helipads, tanks and
   green roofs across a large map.
2. The roof stays mostly the box's color: what it carries covers a minority of
   it, and a green roof is the box's color with some green in it.
