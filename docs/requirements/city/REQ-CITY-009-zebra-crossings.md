## Statement

A street along which two obstacles face each other for more than 1.6 units
**shall** carry a zebra crossing where the facing obstacles end.

## Rationale

Crossings mark the junctions of long streets.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A long street shows a zebra crossing at each end of its straight stretch.
