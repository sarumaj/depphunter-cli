## Statement

Each side of a terrace at least 1.5 units long **shall** show a staircase near
one end, the other end being where a ramp may run.

## Rationale

Stairs and ramps show where the levels connect.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A terrace's long side shows a staircase near one end.
