## Statement

Where a bridge (REQ-CITY-026) comes ashore beside the terrace standing on that
shore, a ramp as wide as the deck **shall** carry its road on, straight from
the deck's end and level with it, up the shore to the terrace's top at its
wall, and a driveway **shall** cross the terrace's sidewalk into its ring road.
The ramp **shall** have a wall with a parapet down each side and the deck's
markings on its road; no tree, bush or lamp **shall** stand on it. A bridge
that lands beyond the end of the terrace's side keeps to the shore.

## Rationale

A bridge landed on the shore's lawn, a terrace's height below the streets it
was built to reach: from the deck there was no way on but a jump. The bridges
and the streets are one road network.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Walking off a bridge leads up onto the ring road of the terrace on that
   shore without a jump.
2. The road up from a bridge is as wide as the deck and has nothing planted
   on it.
