## Statement

Each building **shall** have the facade of its type (REQ-CITY-031) - framed
windows and French windows onto balconies, a glass curtain wall, English-bond
brick with lintels, sills and a toothed cornice, precast panels with their seams,
art-deco piers and spandrels, ribbed cladding with clerestory windows, or a
stone shop floor under flats - with window bays per 0.3-unit story, a ground
floor with a door (shopfronts under awnings, a lobby, or loading doors) and a
cornice or coping on top.

## Rationale

Varied facades make a building recognizable as a building and tell buildings
apart.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Buildings show all seven facades across a large map.
2. Every building has a door on its ground floor.
