## Statement

Each building **shall** pick a facade style - brick with framed windows and
sills, concrete panels, or, for tall buildings, a glass curtain wall - with
window bays per 0.3-unit storey, a ground-floor shopfront with a door and a
cornice on top.

## Rationale

Varied facades make a building recognisable as a building and tell buildings
apart.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Buildings show at least the three facade styles across a large map.
2. Every building has a door on its ground floor.
