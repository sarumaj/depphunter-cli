## Statement

The nail gun **shall** reach across a street and no further (9 units) and its
nails **shall** scatter.

## Rationale

The nail gun is built for the near end of the map and hoses a wall.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The nail gun's reach is 9 units.
2. Nails aimed at one point land spread around it.
