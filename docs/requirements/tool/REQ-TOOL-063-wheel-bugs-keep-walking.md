## Statement

While the wheel is up, the bugs **shall** continue walking their laps.

## Rationale

Two hundred bugs stopping mid-stride is worse to watch, and with bites frozen
none of them can charge for the pause.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Bugs visibly move behind the open wheel.
