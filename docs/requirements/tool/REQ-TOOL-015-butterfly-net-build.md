## Statement

The butterfly net **shall** have a bound grip, a ferrule and a bag with
stiffening rings, on a hoop standing in the line of the shaft.

## Rationale

All tools are built to the same standard of detail.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The net shows a bound grip, a ferrule, a hoop and a bag drawn as netting with
   rings.
