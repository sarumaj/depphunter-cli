## Statement

Closing the hand around a held tool **shall** bend each finger at every joint,
the knuckle least and the middle joint most.

## Rationale

A hand does not fold evenly; that is the difference between a fist and a claw.

## Acceptance criteria

1. For a given grip, the intermediate phalanx is turned furthest and the
   proximal least.
