## Statement

While the water skimmers are in hand and working, open water **shall** hold the
walker up as a floor.

## Rationale

The bay becomes a street and the islands stop being somewhere only a bridge
reaches.

## Acceptance criteria

1. With the skimmers in hand the walker walks over the bay.
2. Only the skimmers float the walker.
