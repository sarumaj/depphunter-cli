## Statement

While the swim ring is worn and working, open water **shall** hold the walker
up, swimming: down in the water to the chest, the ring round it on the surface
and the head out over the water, going slower than on foot. The body
**shall** swim the way and as hard as the walker goes, the ring kept flat on
the water however it leans: treading water, upright, the legs pedalling and the
hands on the ring; swimming forward, leant into the ring, a flutter kick behind
and the hands paddling before it; flat out, leant further, kicking harder and
the arms coming over the ring in a crawl; backward, leant back, the legs
pedalling ahead; sideways, over to that side, the legs swept away from it and
the arm on that side reaching out - whatever the hands hold going with them.
The off hand **shall** hold the ring's pacifier on its cord, the same seen from
the eye and from behind, and the ring **shall** be
seen round the chest from behind and looking down. Coming ashore and going in
**shall** happen over a moment, not as a jump of the view, and a swimmer
**shall not** jump: there is nothing to push off. A choice of tool
saved as the water skimmers, the ring's old name, **shall** be read as the
ring.

## Rationale

The islands stop being somewhere only a bridge reaches. A swimmer in a ring is
something anyone recognizes, and something to hold in the off hand - the
pacifier - keeps what is in the hand the same from the eye and from behind.

## Acceptance criteria

1. With the ring on the walker swims across the bay, slower than walking.
2. Only the ring keeps the walker up in the water.
3. Swimming, the view is low over the water and, from behind, the walker is in
   the water to the chest with the ring round it and the pacifier in the left
   hand; ashore again, the view and the body come back up over a moment.
4. A tool saved as `skimmers` comes back as the ring.
5. Space does nothing while swimming; ashore, it jumps again.
6. Seen from behind, treading water, swimming, swimming flat out (Shift),
   backward and sideways each look different: the arms rest on the ring,
   paddle before it and come over it in a crawl.
