## Statement

Taking from the wheel a tool that is already in hand **shall** keep it in hand.

## Rationale

The wheel has a wedge for the bare hand; a cursor that drifted onto the jet is
not a reason to fall out of the sky.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Taking the jet backpack from the wheel while flying with it keeps the walker
   flying.
