## Statement

The fishing rod **shall** have a cork grip, a tapering blank, line guides and a
reel with a handle.

## Rationale

All tools are built to the same standard of detail.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The rod shows a cork grip, a blank thinning to the tip, guide rings and a
   reel with a handle.
