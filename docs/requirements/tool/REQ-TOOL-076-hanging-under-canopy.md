## Statement

Under the canopy the view **shall** swing with the walker: rolling with the
lines in a turn, nodding as the canopy surges or is flared, and dipping as the
lines take the opening jolt, all damped so that a swing dies away; where the
page asks for reduced motion it **shall not**. The left hand **shall** pull its
toggle down as far as the left side of the canopy is pulled - the brakes, a turn
to the left, a flare.

## Rationale

Hanging from lines is not standing on the ground, and the hand on the toggle is
how the walker sees what the keys are doing.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Steady in a turn, the walker's swing leans with the bank.
2. After the opening jolt the lines settle back to their length in steady
   flight.
