## Statement

Using the jet backpack **shall** give a burst of thrust (0.9 s at three times
the speed) rather than fire anything.

## Rationale

Opening the throttle is the jet's use.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Using the jet backpack while flying speeds the walker up for about a second.
