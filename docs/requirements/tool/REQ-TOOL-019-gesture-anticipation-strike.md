## Statement

A tool gesture **shall** consist of an anticipation, a strike and a
follow-through rather than a linear ramp, and the hand **shall** grip harder
through it.

## Rationale

That is what makes a throw look thrown rather than slid.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A swung gesture first moves backwards, then drives through, then settles to
   rest.
2. The fingers close further during the gesture than at rest.
