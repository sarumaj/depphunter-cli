## Statement

The butterfly net **shall** be swung up over the shoulder on the wind-up and
down across the body to finish low on the other side, with the hoop turning so
that the mouth leads.

## Rationale

That is how a net is swung.

## Acceptance criteria

1. During a swing the hoop starts high right and ends low left.
