## Statement

Each tool that throws **shall** throw its own projectile: the rod a bobber
trailing its line, the bubble wand a wobbling bubble, the dart gun a dart
pointing along its flight, the nail gun a nail, the extinguisher a swelling gout
of foam and the grapple gun a claw trailing its line. The camera, the net, the
jet backpack and the water skimmers **shall** throw nothing.

## Rationale

What flies has to look like what the tool is; the camera's photograph arrives
the moment it is taken.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A cast shows a red-and-white bobber with a line back to the rod tip.
2. A dart and a nail point along their direction of flight.
3. Using the camera or the net sends nothing into the scene.
