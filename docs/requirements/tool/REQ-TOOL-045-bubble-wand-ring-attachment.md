## Statement

The bubble wand's ring **shall** be attached to the stick at its rim, lifted by
its own radius, with a collar over the joint.

## Rationale

Centred on the tip the stick runs through the ring, which reads as a ring
threaded onto a stick.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The stick ends at the bottom of the ring's rim, covered by a collar.
