## Statement

The bubble wand **shall** have a bottle cap and a soap film across its ring that
thins and lets go as the wand is waved.

## Rationale

All tools are built to the same standard of detail.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The wand shows a bottle cap, a stick, a ring and a translucent film.
2. The film's opacity drops during the wave and returns afterwards.
