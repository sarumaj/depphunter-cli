## Statement

The tracking dart **shall** have the longest reach of the primary tools, 60
units - about as far as it can be lobbed into a gusting wind (REQ-TOOL-081) -
**shall** be aimed through the scope, **shall** fire one shot per click and
**shall** steer towards a wall up to 50 units ahead of it.

## Rationale

The dart is a marksman's tool. At 34 units it reached barely across a district,
which from a rooftop left most of what the scope showed out of reach.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The dart's reach is more than three times the nail gun's, and an aimed dart
   reaches its mark at that reach in still air and in the wind.
2. Holding the button fires one dart.
