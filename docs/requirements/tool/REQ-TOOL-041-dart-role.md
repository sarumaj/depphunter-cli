## Statement

The tracking dart **shall** have the longest reach of the primary tools,
**shall** be aimed through the scope, **shall** fire one shot per click and
**shall** steer.

## Rationale

The dart is a marksman's tool.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The dart's reach is more than three times the nail gun's.
2. Holding the button fires one dart.
