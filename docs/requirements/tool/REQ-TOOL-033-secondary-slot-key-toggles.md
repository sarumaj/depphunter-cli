## Statement

Pressing the slot key of the secondary tool already in hand **shall** put it
down, leaving the left hand empty.

## Rationale

That is how the walker comes out of the air or off the water deliberately.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Pressing the jet backpack's key while carrying it empties the left hand and
   the walker falls.
